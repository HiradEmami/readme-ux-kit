## Composition Recipe

1. Header: `header_cyber_frame.svg`
2. Hero strip: `banner_horizontal_breach.svg`
3. Status block: compact table with live, warning, and success icons
4. Divider: `divider_scanning_bar.svg`
5. Feature grid: icons from `dev`, `devops`, and `status`
6. Closing divider: `divider_red_alert.svg` for security or `divider_neon.svg` for general projects
